What we do

Services shaped around how you live.

From a single room to a full custom home, the process stays clear: listen first, plan carefully, and build with materials that age well.

01

Home renovations

Whole-home and partial renovations that keep the character of the house while improving light, flow, and finish. We coordinate structure, mechanicals, and millwork so the result feels considered, not patched.

02

Kitchens

Layouts that work hard, cabinetry with quiet detail, and surfaces chosen for daily use. A kitchen renovation should feel effortless once you are living in it.

03

Bathrooms

Calm, durable rooms with careful waterproofing, lighting, and storage. We keep the palette restrained so the space can rest.

04

Additions

New rooms that belong to the original house — in proportion, in material, and in how they meet the landscape of South Georgian Bay.

05

Basement renovations

Lower levels finished for living — light, storage, and rooms that feel intentional, not tucked away. Built with care for moisture, access, and how the space connects to the rest of the home.

06

Exterior & decks

Cladding, timber work, and outdoor living spaces built to take snow, sun, and time. The outside of the house should be as resolved as the rooms within.
