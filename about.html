About us

Collaborative building, clear expectations.

Apex Pro Contracting is a boutique general contracting company. We provide a full range of construction services for large or smaller projects.

With over 20 years of construction and construction management experience, we can help you achieve all of your construction and renovation goals.

Our focus is built on building realistic expectations with our clients both in terms of budget and time frame. We want our clients to be actively involved in their project from start to finish.

This collaborative approach allows for more informed decisions and realistic expectations for everyone involved. We want to help our clients achieve their vision and love their new home.
