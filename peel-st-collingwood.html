Overview

Rebuilt from the studs out.

This was a complete gut back to the studs. The house was left in a state of complete disrepair and needed structural repairs, all new services, and complete replacement of all interior and exterior surfaces.

Every service was replaced: a completely new electrical system from the pole, all new plumbing from the street, a new HVAC system, and a new natural gas hook-up. This project was completed in 2025.

Every project is built with care, clear communication, and craftsmanship that holds up over time.

Scope of work
  • Complete gut back to the studs
  • Structural repairs
  • New electrical from the pole — panels, wiring, and fixtures
  • New plumbing from the street — pipes, fixtures, and hot water tank
  • New HVAC — furnace, ductwork, and registers
  • New natural gas hook-up
  • New interior surfaces throughout
  • New exterior surfaces throughout
